About

Hi, welcome to my website! I'm Siyue Liu (刘思月), a fifth-year Ph.D. student at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) Tepper School of Business. I'm in the Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization (ACO) Program. I am very fortunate to be advised by Professors Gérard Cornuéjols and R. Ravi. I am broadly interested in combinatorial optimization, approximation algorithms, integer programming and nonconvex optimization.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2022 with a Bachelor in Economics, where Professor Taotao He guided me to the world of optimization. I was a research intern at Microsoft Research, Redmond, in the Algorithms group in Summer 2025, mentored by Victor Reis. During my Ph.D., I long-term visited London School of Economics (LSE) in Fall 2023, hosted by Professor Ahmad Abdi, University of Bonn in Fall 2025, hosted by Professor László Végh, and Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Spring 2026, hosted by Professor Kristóf Bérczi. Besides, I'm grateful to the mentorship and guidance from Professors Christopher Thomas Ryan, Tim Huh and Fatma Kılınç-Karzan and many others in the early stage of my career.

I'm on the 2026-2027 academic job market, looking for tenure-track faculty, research scientist, and postdoc positions.
